Community benefits

Formal CBA = a signed, legally enforceable community benefits agreement meeting all six criteria: enforceable contract; defined community-side signatory; specific, measurable commitments; governance and reporting mechanisms; remedies for non-performance; negotiated before approvals were granted.

Tax abatements, development agreements, MOAs and voluntary grant programs do not qualify.

Nationwide there are just 2 such agreements: the City of Lancaster, PA agreement (one document covering three data centers across two campuses) and the St. Louis Armory agreement — together 4 of 1,185 tracked projects.
Documented benefits = projects where publicly reported community commitments were found — grants, community funds, infrastructure spending, workforce programs, water or energy commitments.

Most are discretionary corporate giving: real money, but revocable at the company's discretion and not enforceable by the community. Blank means researched-and-none-found or not yet researched (see Methodology).
